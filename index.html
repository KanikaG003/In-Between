about

I work at Rockpecker Private Limited, where my days move between finance, funding and investment, the group's global expansion across India and overseas, contract review and negotiation, and marketing.

I hold an MBA in Finance from Chitkara University, with a superspecialisation in corporate finance, investment banking and wealth management through the CXOs NextGen Centre of Excellence in Finance, and a B.Com (Hons.) in Accounting and Finance from the same university.

My path has run from a finance desk in Haryana to Disney's Hollywood Studios in Florida. Through all of it, I write. When I'm not writing, I'm sketching, dancing or cooking.
